# Landing Page Structure

## Rule zero: the visitor decides in 5 seconds

A non-technical visitor must answer three questions without scrolling:
1. What is this? 2. Is it for someone like me? 3. What do I do next?

## Proven section order (service / agency / SaaS)

1. **Sticky header** — logo, 4–5 links max, one primary CTA.
2. **Hero** — one clear promise + one supporting sentence + 2 CTAs + product visual.
3. **Proof strip** — 3–4 stats or trust logos. Short, one line each.
4. **Services** — 3 cards is the sweet spot. Each: icon, name, one-line benefit, 3–5 concrete outcomes, CTA.
5. **Self-qualification** — "Which solution do you need?" interactive chooser.
6. **Portfolio / case study** — filterable, visual, with a detail modal.
7. **Process** — 4 steps, numbered, plain language.
8. **FAQ** — 5–6 real objections, accordion.
9. **Final CTA** — repeat the promise + one action.
10. **Footer** — contact, links, legal.

## Copy rules

- Headline: benefit first, jargon never. "کسب‌وکارت رو هوشمندتر مدیریت کن" beats "AI-driven data orchestration".
- One idea per sentence. Max ~18 words.
- Replace abstractions with examples: not "automation" alone but "گزارش روزانه خودکار".
- Every section ends with a next step (scroll cue or CTA).
- Never Lorem Ipsum; use realistic domain content.
- Say what it is NOT: "این‌ها نمونه‌های نمایشی هستند".

## CTA hierarchy

- One primary action repeated (WhatsApp / demo request).
- Secondary actions are quieter (ghost buttons, text links).
- CTA text = verb + outcome: "مشاوره رایگان در واتساپ", not "ارسال".

## Visual rhythm

Alternate background tones (white / near-white) between sections.
Keep vertical spacing generous: 96–128px desktop, 56–72px mobile.
One focal point per screen — never two competing heroes.

## Accessibility + trust

- Real content, no lorem. Visible phone/WhatsApp everywhere.
- Mark demo content clearly.
- Keyboard-operable nav, menus, modals, accordions.
